## What is LIVE on the site

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Portfolio homepage |
| `services.html` | Freelance website & SEO service plans (linked as `/services`) |
| `daauc/` | The daauc page — kept live on purpose |

## What is SAVED but NOT published

All projects, single-page websites and demos are stored in this repo for
safekeeping only. They are listed in the `exclude` section of `_config.yml`,
so GitHub Pages never serves them. **Nothing is deleted**, and any item can
be made live again by removing its line from `_config.yml`.

Full index: [`_archive/README.md`](_archive/README.md)

Archived sections: `projects/`, `demos/`, `experiments/`, `niss/` (the full
Noor Infotech website copy), `1/`, `LITHOS/`, `KRO-FLITE/`, `STUDIO/`,
`NINJUTSU-CREATIVE/`, `neo/`, and the old root pages `Aurora.html`,
`noor.html`, `niss-service.html`, `qr-cloud.html`, `bd.html`.
